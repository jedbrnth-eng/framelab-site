Privacy Policy

FRAME-LAB for iPhone · Effective October 14, 2026

Short version: FRAME-LAB does not collect any data about you. The app works entirely on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no servers, and it does not send your photos or any other information over the internet.

Camera

FRAME-LAB asks for camera access so it can show a live preview and take photos with the camera and film look you choose. Camera frames are processed on your device only and are never uploaded.

Photos

FRAME-LAB asks for permission to add photos to your library, so it can save the pictures you develop. It does not ask to read your library.

When you choose “Choose from library”, Apple’s system photo picker opens. FRAME-LAB only receives the photos you pick in that picker, nothing else. When it develops an imported photo it keeps only the original capture date and time; it does not keep location data from the original.

Data stored on your device

FRAME-LAB keeps some data on your iPhone so the app works properly:

  • Your settings and preferences (for example the last camera and film you used, look strength and favourite edits).
  • A temporary copy of each shot until it has been saved to your Photos library, so a photo is not lost if the app is interrupted. It is deleted once the save succeeds.

This data never leaves your device through FRAME-LAB. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, under Apple’s terms. Deleting the app deletes it.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information, usage data, diagnostics, identifiers or location. FRAME-LAB contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

If you choose to share crash reports with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to us under Apple’s own privacy policy. If you join the TestFlight beta, Apple shares the feedback you choose to send, plus basic tester information described in Apple’s TestFlight terms.

Children

FRAME-LAB does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If FRAME-LAB ever starts collecting any data, this page will say so before that version is released.
